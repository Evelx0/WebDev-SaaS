Process

A reusable simple sales flow.

This structure works well for many service businesses: discovery, quote, build, launch. Keep it short and believable.

  1. 01

    Discovery

    Define the problem, audience, scope, and success criteria in plain language.

  2. 02

    Quote

    Set expectations, timeline, deliverables, and what is explicitly out of scope.

  3. 03

    Build

    Ship in small visible steps, review often, and keep stakeholders close to the work.

  4. 04

    Launch

    Deploy cleanly, verify the essentials, and define what happens after handover.
